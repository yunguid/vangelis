# Replicas

Pieces Luke finds special enough to rebuild inside Vangelis, note by note, from sounds we
make or find ourselves, and to spend real time and compute on until they stand next to the
record. Each one keeps a journey log: what was tried, what was measured, what was kept.

| Piece | Record | Built from | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pernambuco | Luiz Bonfá, *Solo in Rio 1959* (Smithsonian Folkways SFW40483) | a string-per-channel transcription played from the University of Iowa guitar recordings, voiced like the record | on the site, opens the page; toolkit in `scripts/guitar-transcription` |
| The Shade of the Mango Tree | Luiz Bonfá with Don Burrows and George Golla, *Bonfa Burrows Brazil* (1978) | Bonfá's guitar alone, lifted out of the band by source separation, then transcribed and played like Pernambuco; the flute, bass, drums and strings are not played | on the site, in the landing queue: [journey](shade-of-the-mango-tree/JOURNEY.md); toolkit in `scripts/guitar-transcription` |
| Blade Runner Blues | Vangelis, *Blade Runner* (Music From The Original Soundtrack), EastWest 1994 | the app's own synthesizer, CS-80 patches designed from the record, playing an MPE-style transcription | v2 on the site, in the landing queue; its sounds on the dial: [journey](blade-runner-blues/JOURNEY.md); toolkit in `scripts/synth-transcription` |

The records themselves never enter the repo: they are fetched into a scratch folder for
analysis, and only notes, parameters and measurements leave it.
